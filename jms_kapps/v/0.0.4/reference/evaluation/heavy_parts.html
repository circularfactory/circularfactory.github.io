jms_kapps.evaluation.heavy_parts#

The heavy parts of a spec with the precision rule, in its rounds (interim names): which repetition of each the rounds make, and the repetitions a person asks for. Pure functions over the run summaries, with no store and no clock.

A heavy part is one of three:

  • a ramp (spec, ramp), named by its block: its levels, one run each, in rising order of their box rates, until the ramp ends by its rule;

  • a replay (replay_levels), named by the number of its configuration: the levels of the store-only baseline that replay the configuration’s first valid UC2 run, one for each factor in rising order, until the first broken level. A replay at the factor 1 runs at normal load, but it follows the same rules;

  • a sustained load, named by the number of its configuration: a heavy configuration (heavy = true) that is no ramp’s level, one run. A heavy configuration with replay factors is a sustained load and a replay.

Each runs one time, in round 1. The rounds (rounds) make the first repetition of each heavy part in round 1, at its place in the spec’s order: a ramp at its block’s place, a sustained load at its configuration’s place, and a replay right after its configuration’s first valid run. A configuration with no valid run keeps its replay for the round that makes one. Then a person reads the result and decides if a repetition is necessary. A later round makes no level of a heavy part whose repetition ended (Standing.ended): a ramp that ended by its rule or reached its top level, a replay that broke, failed or reached its highest factor, a sustained load with a record. One that a round left unfinished (a stop, a run that did not complete, a level skipped for want of an approval) goes on in the next round, from its lowest level without a record.

A repetition on request (requested_repetitions, the option REPEAT_OPTION): a call names each heavy part with a target repetition, a ramp by its block (block3:2), a replay or a sustained load by its configuration’s number (7:2); for a heavy configuration with replay factors the number names both. The target asks the rounds to make sure that repetition exists: while the part’s last repetition ended below the target, the next round makes one more (Standing.after), a ramp again from its lowest rate, a replay again from its lowest factor, each until it ends by its rule. A target at or below a repetition that exists or is under way makes nothing, so a restart with the same command line is safe. A repetition has its own number in its records (sweep.repetition 2, then 3), and its runs take the pick seed of that repetition. A repeated replay replays the same run as its first repetition did (first_replayed), so the repetitions show the store and the time of day, with the same input.
