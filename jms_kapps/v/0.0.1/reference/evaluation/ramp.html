jms_kapps.evaluation.ramp#

The broken rule of the measurement harness, and how far a ramp went (iteration one; decided by Etienne on 2026-10-11, the numbers decision, jms_kapps 56; the stop rule and the judgement changed on 2026-10-12, jms_kapps 99, decided by Sören Weindel as the product owner of the tests, jms_kapps 85).

A ramp is a sweep block marked as a ramp (ramp = true), whose only axis is its rising box rates (spec). Each rate is one load level, made by one run. Its runs are heavy, so a ramp runs only under an approval that covers its spec for the period (approval; any person may approve, jms_kapps 96). The sweep runs a ramp one rate per run, in rising order, judges each level with the broken rule after its run, and stops the ramp at the first level it ends at (sweep).

The broken rule. A load level is broken at the first store-side signal, from its runs of use case 2 with the wrapper (uc2), each over its measured window but the first:

  • a refusal for want of heap (heap refusal), under the load: in the warm-up, the window and the drain (summary);

  • more than 1 % of the store requests refused or unanswered, timed out or with their connection dropped (refused or unanswered; until 2026-10-14 named refused or timed out, the same sum, jms_kapps 109);

  • a p95 commit latency more than 10 times its value at the ramp’s lowest rate, the lowest box rate of the same configuration (p95 commit latency).

A fourth signal, a plateau (plateau: the completed writes per minute rose by less than 5 % from the rate step before), is still found and shown in the record of the level, but it breaks no level and stops no ramp: a plateau alone cannot tell whether the store, KAPPS or UC2 is the limit.

Stuck or failed boxes alone never break a level: they are counted for each level, beside it, and UC2’s failures are no store-side signal. A level whose runs hold several repetitions pools them: the shares and the p95 over the requests and the commits of all of them, the writes per minute as their median.

UC2-limited. A level is UC2-limited when fewer than 90 % of the spawns offered in its window are accepted (ACCEPTED_SHARE, a judgement setting outside the configuration’s key). Offered spawns are the spawned and the refused ones, counted in the measured window from the WarehouseManagementSystem’s time-stamped output lines (record.spawns_in_window); the accepted share is the accepted over the offered. A UC2-limited level breaks nothing: the ramp goes on.

The end of a ramp. A ramp ends at its first broken level, and its scale is the level before it; none where the ramp broke at its lowest rate. A ramp also ends at a level that has a run but no run that counts (a run that failed, or one that a check found invalid): such a level cannot be judged, and the ramp sends no higher rate to the store. A level whose only run a stop signal ended has not run yet, and runs again. A ramp none of whose levels broke has no scale yet: it was not broken up to its highest rate. Each repetition of a ramp, a repetition on request in a spec with the precision rule (heavy_parts, jms_kapps 114), is judged apart, from its own runs.

The judgement of a ramp is one of three. UC2-limited at level X when the first UC2-limited level comes before the first broken level, or when no level broke. Broken at level Y (signal) when a level broke before any UC2-limited level. Cannot tell when neither happened (only a plateau, or the top level reached, or no spawn counts in the records), or both happened at the same level. The table compares a UC2-limited ramp with the replay of the same configuration (ramp_comparison).

(Added on 2026-10-12, jms_kapps 49.)

(Changed on 2026-10-12, jms_kapps 99, decided by Sören Weindel as the product owner of the tests, jms_kapps 85. Until then a level was broken on four signals, a plateau of the completed writes among them, and the ramp stopped at its first broken level, so a UC2-limited level, which usually plateaus, stopped the ramp and its plateau said nothing about the store or KAPPS. The plateau is now only shown; the judgement and the accepted share are new.)
