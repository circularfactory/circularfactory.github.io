jms_kapps.uc2.transport#

The job of carrying one box, as the graph records it: one BoxTransportOperation for each box.

The transport names the box, its origin and its destination, and the box links back to its transport (fc:hasTransport): the opening writes both, so a party that holds a box finds the transport through the box, never from the box’s name. Its status is the middleware’s svc:operationStatus, and it says where the journey is:

  • queued from the moment it is opened, in the commit that creates the box on its origin, until the origin is notified;

  • running once the origin was notified and carries the box on;

  • done once the box is back in the WarehouseManagementSystem’s possession, written in the same commit that makes it the possessor;

  • failed when the party that cannot go on ends it: on the station the module it is assigned to, or the WarehouseManagementSystem when it cannot notify the origin. Its svc:executionResult says why.

(Changed on 2026-10-06, jms_kapps 41, decided by Etienne: the WarehouseManagementSystem creates the box on its origin. Until then queued lasted “while the box is still in the WarehouseManagementSystem or has just reached its origin”, the WarehouseManagementSystem ended a transport “when its handover to the origin is refused”, and the paragraph below named one exception, “the party that has just passed both on and then cannot reach the receiving module”, and the box’s first possession was the WarehouseManagementSystem’s.)

A transport is assigned to one module at a time: the one that carries the box on now. Core’s cfc:executedOn names it, and cfc:implementsCapability names that module’s :BoxTransportCapability. The WarehouseManagementSystem opens the transport assigned to the origin, on which it creates the box. At each hop both move to the next module in the entry commit of the handover that passes the box, so the assigned module is also the box’s possessor, and only it writes the transport on the station: a module that has just passed both on and then cannot reach the receiving module writes nothing, and the receiving module ends the transport. The WarehouseManagementSystem writes it only in the create that opens it and when it cannot notify the origin. The box has no state of its own.

(Changed on 2026-10-06, jms_kapps 42, decided by Etienne: only the assigned module writes the transport. Until then failed also came from “the module that passed the box and the transport on when it cannot reach the receiving module after the move”, and the paragraph above read “The exceptions are the module that has just passed both on and then cannot reach the receiving module, which ends the transport failed, since nobody else would, and the WarehouseManagementSystem, which does the same when it cannot notify the origin.”)

Every write of a transport is a commit of the middleware: one mw.create() opens it together with the box and the box’s first possession, by the origin, each change of status is a mw.commit(), and each reassignment and the last status, done, join the entry commit of a handover.

The writes of a box’s transport, each a commit of the middleware mw.

A write the store refuses raises the triplestore interface’s ShaclValidationError, which the middleware passes on unchanged; the caller catches that type.

open(box: IRI, *, origin: IRI, destination: IRI, capability: IRI, possessor: IRI) → IRI[source]#

Open the transport of a new box, queued, in one commit with the box itself and its first possession by possessor, and return the transport’s IRI.

The opening chooses the transport’s IRI, and writes the link both ways: the transport names its box (fc:hasBox), and the box its transport (fc:hasTransport). So a party that holds the box finds the transport through the box, and needs no rule that names a transport after its box.

The transport is assigned to origin, and implements capability, the origin’s BoxTransportCapability. The box points at the possessor’s one possession state, which the same commit creates when the possessor has none yet.

Raises:

  • kapps_triplestore_interface.PreconditionFailedError – If the box or its transport is in the graph already (from the object-graph mapper’s checked write), and nothing is written.

  • kapps_triplestore_interface.ShaclValidationError – If the store refuses the commit, with its validation report, and nothing is written.
