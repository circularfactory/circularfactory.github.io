jms_kapps.evaluation.rounds#

The rounds of a sweep under the precision rule (interim names): which runs the next round makes. Pure functions over the run summaries, with no store and no clock.

A round makes one run of each selected configuration under the rule that is still open, in the spec’s order: round k makes repetition k. A configuration is open while it does not meet the rule and has made fewer than the rule’s maximum of runs, 20 (is_open, the one place that decides it); one whose throughput median is 0 once it has its minimum of valid runs stops there. A round is a time window in the sense of the numbers decision, so the minimum of 10 valid runs comes from at least 10 time windows.

The next round is the lowest round that has a run to make: a repetition without a record of an open selected configuration (next_round). A run has a record when it completed or failed; an interrupted attempt has none, so its repetition is planned again. Everything is read from the records (the summaries): a call that stops goes on at its round in the next call, a configuration with more records than the others is planned in the round of its next repetition, and the records made in the passes before the rounds count as their repetitions.

jms_kapps.evaluation.rounds.next_round(found: Sequence[SpecConfiguration], rule: PrecisionRule, summaries: Sequence[RunSummary], *, configuration_numbers: Collection[int] | None = None, repetition_numbers: Collection[int] | None = None) → Round | None[source]#

The lowest round that has a run to make among the open selected configurations, with its runs, or None where there is none: every selected configuration meets the rule, has made its maximum or stopped at a throughput median of 0, or, with repetition_numbers, every selected repetition of each open one has a record.

Parameters:

  • found – The spec’s configurations; only those under the rule (ruled) are planned.

  • configuration_numbers – The configurations selected by their numbers, or None for all.

  • repetition_numbers – The repetitions selected by their numbers, or None for all: a call narrowed to them makes only their rounds (–repetitions).
